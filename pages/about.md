# About

## A notebook, with the door open

The Way I AI is where I collect, organize, and record my thoughts about working with AI, especially when it comes to building software.

It started as something for myself: a place to put ideas so I could find them again, make sense of them, and see how they change. Then I thought: why not do it in public? Probably no other human will read it anyway.

If you are another human: welcome. There goes that theory.

## Who’s behind this?

I’m [tkarakai](https://github.com/tkarakai), a software engineer and architect tinkering with agentic development. I love the process of learning, trying things, and gradually getting more productive—even when learning something new involves briefly becoming less productive first.

This collection is a record of that process, not a claim that I’ve figured it all out. It’s called *The Way I AI*, not *The Way Everyone Should AI*.

## And how much of this is AI?

Quite a lot. It would be a slightly odd subject to write about while pretending otherwise.

The ideas originate with me. AI contributes heavily to developing and expressing them, but I edit, review, and iterate until the result reflects what I wanted to convey. Sometimes that takes a few rounds. Sometimes “a few” is doing a lot of work in that sentence.

The words get help. The judgment, and responsibility for what I publish, stays with me.

## What’s up with the animals?

No particular reason. Sometimes I need to remind myself that nature is out there, beyond the screen and the terminal. And I like this style of wildlife illustration.
